# 🚨 Disaster Management System – Web App

A web-based **Disaster Management System** designed to provide **real-time alerts, SOS assistance, and emergency coordination** during natural disasters such as floods, cyclones, landslides, and fires.

This project focuses on improving **communication, response speed, and resource management** during emergencies.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

During disasters, people often face:
- Delayed alerts
- Misinformation
- Poor coordination between authorities and citizens

This system provides a **centralized platform** to deliver verified information, manage emergency requests, and support faster response.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Provide **location-based disaster alerts**
- Enable **SOS requests with live location**
- Share **verified announcements**
- Assist authorities in monitoring disasters
- Improve coordination between citizens and volunteers

---

## 🧩 Features

### 🚨 Emergency Alerts
- Location-based alerts (district / GPS)
- Severity levels: Low, Medium, High, Critical
- SMS / Email / Push notification support

### 🗺️ Live Disaster Map
- Shows affected areas, safe zones, shelters, and hospitals
- Color-coded severity indicators

### 🆘 SOS & Help Requests
- One-click SOS with GPS location
- Help types: Rescue, Medical, Food, Shelter
- Request status tracking

### 🏥 Shelter & Resource Management
- Shelter listing with capacity tracking
- Food, medicine, and emergency resources

### 📢 Official Announcements
- Admin-only verified updates
- Evacuation notices and safety instructions

---

## 🧠 Advanced Features

- Citizen reporting with images/videos
- Volunteer coordination
- Role-based access (Citizen / Volunteer / Admin)
- Multi-language support
- Offline emergency guidelines
- Dark mode for power outages
