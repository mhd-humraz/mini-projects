Welcome to Maplewood Community School

Founded in 1985, Maplewood Community School has been dedicated to providing exceptional education that fosters intellectual curiosity, creativity, and character development.

Our holistic approach to learning ensures that each student receives personalized attention and opportunities to explore their interests while building a strong academic foundation.

We believe in creating a supportive community where students, teachers, and parents collaborate to create the best possible learning environment.
